Questions

Most common topics.

How are cohorts assembled?

We group by industry and experience level. Corporate clients can reserve private pods. Solo learners join curated cohorts that match their goals.

What’s the time commitment?

~5 hours per week: 2 hours async lessons, 1 hour pod workshop, 1 hour quizzes, 1 hour reflection/office hours.

Do you support corporate invoicing?

Yes. We integrate with Square/Stripe plus custom PO flows. Multi-seat licenses can be split across business units.

Can we white-label the platform?

Enterprise plans can co-brand dashboards, email templates, and certificates. Full white-label is available via SOW.

What certifications are covered?

PMP, PMI-ACP, PgMP, and Disciplined Agile tracks. PM Elite Skills focuses on PMI-aligned frameworks with original question banks.

Do mentors provide 1:1 coaching?

Every learner gets two 1:1 sessions plus unlimited async threads via the portal. Extra hours can be added for executive coaching.
